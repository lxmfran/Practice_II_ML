# `AI_USAGE.md` — generative-AI disclosure

**Team:** Team 1 · **Cumulative:** one section per milestone, added as you go.

Generative-AI assistance is **permitted, with disclosure and accountability**.

For each milestone, state four things:

- the **tool or service** used;
- the **purpose**;
- the **files or substantive sections** affected;
- **how the output was checked**.

**If no generative AI was used in a milestone, one sentence saying so is sufficient.**

**Complete chat histories and prompt logs are not required** and should not be submitted.

## What AI may and may not do here

AI may support brainstorming, explanation, debugging, coding suggestions, language review and
similar work.

**The team remains fully responsible for** correctness, references, code, interpretation,
reproducibility, and understanding everything submitted.

**No generative-AI assistance is permitted during the individual defence questioning.**
Institutional academic-integrity rules continue to apply.

A missing or empty file is a *Reproducibility and craft* deduction. A declaration that the
defence contradicts is an academic-integrity matter rather than a marking one — the question
at the defence is not *whether* AI was used, but whether you understand what was submitted.
